One line, 147 characters, in English: the elevator pitch a city pigeon gives
investors for its startup.

> I'm raising a seed round — literally. I'm the city's fastest coo-rier: lunch delivered by beak before your elevator dings, and I work for crumbs.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) reads `entry.txt` next to it,
validates it — one line, 1–200 characters — and writes it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).

```sh
node main.ts
```

## Why this pitch

- **On subject:** a city pigeon pitching investors for its startup, in its own voice.
- **The joke lands twice:** a "seed round" is literal birdseed, and the fastest
  "coo-rier" in the city delivers lunch by beak before the elevator dings.
- **Pigeon economics:** it works for crumbs — the cheapest runway in the city.
